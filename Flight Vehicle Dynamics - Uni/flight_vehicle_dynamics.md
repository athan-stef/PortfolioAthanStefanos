# Commercial Jet Design Project - Stick Bug
I completed this large group project in the penultimate year of my Aerospace engineering degree. The task was to design a commercial jet targeting the aviation market for 8000 km harmonic range aircraft. This was done in a group of 10 students.

### Responsibilities
- Group management and leadership
- CAD modelling
- Stability analysis
- Technical report and presentation writing

### Documents
This project taught me how to manage multiple group members within the scope of a project and learn how to effectively meet deliverables and deadlines. Technically I grew my skills in stability analysis and large scale CAD models.
