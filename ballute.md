# High Alttitude Recovery Parachute - Ballute
I completed this design project during my final year at Monash High Powered Rocketry. The ballute is designed to act as the first stage of recovery on a 30 km (100,000 ft) sounding rocket. 

I completed the literature reviews, preliminary, and critical design for this parachute. As I graduated I also began developing testing and manufacturing techniques to be used by my successors on this project, which I now offer advisory for. This was quite fun to design and I can't wait to see it fly on Monash High Powered Rocketry's largest rocket!

This project tested my creativity and problem-solving skills. With little guidlines and peer support, I was able to use my engineering skills and innovative mindset to combine literature, analytical methods, and engineering judgement to generate a feasible design.
